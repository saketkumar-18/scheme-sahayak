# PM Ujjwala Yojana 2.0 (PMUY 2.0) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG)
**Scheme ID:** pmuy-2-0 | **Category:** Energy / Clean Cooking

## Overview
PM Ujjwala Yojana (launched 1 May 2016; Ujjwala 2.0 from Aug 2021) provides **deposit-free LPG connections** to adult women of poor households — 10.35+ crore connections issued. From 2023–24, the government added a **₹300 per cylinder DBT subsidy** (up to 12 refills/year) for PMUY beneficiaries.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Applicant is an **adult woman (18+)** of the household — connection issued in her name.
2. Household is **poor** per accepted identification: **BPL / SC / ST / SECC-2011 list / Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) / PMAY beneficiary / kutcha house household / forest dwellers** etc. (as per the eligibility categories on pmuy.gov.in).
3. No existing LPG connection in the household (name-based dedupe).
4. Aadhaar + bank account (for ₹300 DBT subsidy) + mobile number.

## Benefits
- **Free LPG connection**: security deposit, regulator, hose, first refill & stove free under current enrolment package (deposit-free per OMCs).
- **₹300/refill DBT subsidy** (announced Oct 2023, ongoing) — up to 12 refills/year, credited to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.
- EMI options for refill/stove cost (as per OMC schemes).

## Application Process
1. Check eligibility + locate distributor at **pmuy.gov.in**.
2. Apply online (pmuy.gov.in → "Apply for New Connection") or through the nearest LPG distributor/CSC with the Ujjwala KYC form.
3. Upload: Aadhaar (woman), BPL/ration/SECC proof, bank passbook, photo, mobile.
4. Distributor issues connection deposit-free; Dedupe check via OMC systems.
5. Refills attract ₹300 DBT to the bank account; EMI tracking via distributor.
