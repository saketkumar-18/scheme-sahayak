# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance (DEA); operated via Post Offices & authorised banks
**Scheme ID:** sukanya-samriddhi | **Category:** Savings / Girl Child

## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (launched 22 Jan 2015 under Beti Bachao Beti Padhao) is a small-savings account for the **girl child** with the **highest interest rate among small savings** (~8.2% p.a., revised quarterly), **EEE tax status** (exempt contribution, interest, maturity).

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Account for a **girl child below 10 years** of age (opened & operated by guardian).
2. Maximum **2 accounts per family** (exception: twins/triplets on second birth).
3. Minimum deposit **₹250/year**, maximum **₹1.5 lakh/year** (deposit window 15 years; maturity 21 years).
4. Account transferable anywhere in India; girl operates account herself at 18+ (passbook based).

## Benefits
- ~8.2% p.a. interest (quarterly notified; among the highest for fixed income).
- **EEE** — deposits eligible for 80C deduction; interest + maturity fully exempt.
- **Partial withdrawal up to 50%** of balance at 18 for education; **premature closure allowed for marriage after 18**.
- If the girl marries before 21, the account closes that month; on her death, the corpus goes to the guardian.

## Application Process
1. Visit nearest post office or authorised bank branch with the girl's **birth certificate**, guardian's KYC (Aadhaar/PAN), and address proof.
2. Fill the SSY account opening form (Form-SSY-1); initial deposit ₹250+.
3. Deposits: up to ₹1.5 lakh/year for 15 years (missed year can be revived with ₹250 + penalty ₹50).
4. Passbook issued; online transfers available via IPPB/some banks.
5. At 21 years: maturity proceeds to the girl; withdrawal rules per above.
