# Ayushman Bharat – PM Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare / National Health Authority (NHA)
**Scheme ID:** ab-pmjay | **Category:** Health

## Overview
AB-PMJAY is the world's largest publicly funded health assurance scheme: **₹5 lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation at 29,000+ empanelled hospitals (public and private), fully cashless. It targets the bottom ~40% of India's population identified through SECC-2011 deprivation and occupational criteria plus Rutgemric category (R1-R5) for urban workers.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. **SECC-2011 based** (rural): deprivation criteria D1–D7 (e.g., only one room with kucha roof, no adult member 18–59, female-headed household no adult male, SC/ST households, landless households deriving income from casual labour).
2. **SECC-2011 based (urban)**: occupational categories — rag pickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors/cobblers, home-based workers, transport workers, sanitation workers, etc.
3. **Ayushman Vay Vandana (29 Oct 2024)**: **every senior citizen aged 70+** gets the ₹5 lakh cover (shared within the eligible 70+ family members) **irrespective of income**, if not covered under comparable public/private health insurance or ESIC.
4. No cap on family size, age, or gender; pre-existing diseases covered from day 1.

## Exclusions
- Income-tax payers were historically not the target base (SECC-based); Vay Vandana explicitly excludes seniors already covered by ESIC or comparable schemes (can opt to continue old scheme or take Vay Vandana).
- As per NHA operational memos, certain constitutional-post holders & government employees in covered categories may be governed by departmental rules for Vay Vandana (only senior citizens covered by CGHS/ESIC have choice parity).

## Benefit
- ₹5,00,000 per family per year, cashless at empanelled hospitals; 1,900+ treatment procedures across 27 specialties.
- Portability: treatment at any empanelled hospital nationwide.

## Application Process
1. Check eligibility/name at beneficiary.nha.gov.in or Ayushman app, or call **14500**.
2. e-KYC with Aadhaar at CSC/portal; download Ayushman Card (ABC).
3. Visit empanelled hospital Ayushman Mitra desk; card + Aadhaar = cashless admission.
4. Grievances: 14500 helpline, state health agency.
