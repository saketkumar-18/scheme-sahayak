# Stand-Up India — Guidance

**Ministry:** Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
**Scheme ID:** stand-up-india | **Category:** Entrepreneurship / Credit

## Overview
Stand-Up India facilitates **bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore** to at least **one Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) borrower** and at least **one woman borrower per bank branch**, for setting up a **greenfield enterprise** — manufacturing, services, trading, or agri-allied activities. Launched 5 April 2016.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Borrower must be **SC, ST, or a woman** (≥18 years).
2. The enterprise must be **greenfield** — a first-time venture of the beneficiary in that sector (manufacturing, services, trading, agri-allied).
3. Loan size: **₹10 lakh – ₹1 crore** (18% margin money expected for new enterprises; composite loan covers term loan + working capital).
4. In case of non-individual entities, **≥51% shareholding** must rest with SC/ST or women promoters.
5. Borrower must not be a defaulter to any bank/financial institution.

## Benefits
- Composite loan (term + working capital) incl. soft supports: margin money requirement can be met via converged schemes; credit guarantee via CGTMSE coverage; handholding support through Stand-Up Mitra / banks / SIDBI.
- Repayment up to 7 years with moratorium of up to 18 months.

## Application Process
1. Register and apply on **standupmitra.in** (SIDBI-run portal) — self or via bank branch.
2. Portal routes to chosen bank branch; UdyamiMitra-style interface for tracking.
3. Upload: identity (Aadhaar/PAN), caste certificate (for SC/ST), project report, address proof, bank details.
4. Bank appraisal → sanction → disbursement; training/incubation via EDI/LSPs (as needed).
5. Track on portal; grievances via bank or SIDBI helpline.
