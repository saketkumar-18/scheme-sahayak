# Post Matric Scholarship for SC Students (PMS-SC) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (DoSEL)
**Scheme ID:** pms-sc | **Category:** Education

## Overview
Centrally Sponsored Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste students — full course fee reimbursement (for notified institutions) + monthly maintenance allowance (hosteller/day-scholar slabs) for **post-matriculation courses** (Class 11 to PhD/professional). Paid via Direct Benefit Transfer to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts through PFMS/NSP.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Student belongs to **Scheduled Caste** (valid caste certificate).
2. Enrolled in a **recognised post-matriculation course** (Class 11 onwards, including ITI, diploma, graduate, postgraduate, professional courses).
3. **Family/guardian income from all sources ≤ ₹2,50,000 per year** (income certificate by competent authority).
4. Scholarship from one source only; not receiving any other scholarship simultaneously (attested by the institution).

## Exclusions
- Diploma-level courses after Class 10 but before +2 level (per NSP definitions) have specific slabs; students already in receipt of full fee waiver may be adjusted.
- Failure in a class for a second time (consecutive) makes the student ineligible for that year.

## Benefit
- Full tuition/course fee (for govt/reputed notified institutions) + maintenance allowance (₹230–₹1,200/month by course level) + book grant.
- DBT via PFMS.

## Application Process (NSP)
1. Register at **scholarships.gov.in** → "New Registration" → get Registration ID on registered mobile.
2. Fill academic, course, institution, bank details; password login.
3. Upload: caste certificate, income certificate, marksheets, bonafide, bank passbook.
4. Institution (Level 1) → District (Level 2) → State/Nodal verification.
5. Merit-cum-means release via PFMS; track "Check Your Status".
