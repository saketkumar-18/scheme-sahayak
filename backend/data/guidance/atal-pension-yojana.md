# Atal Pension Yojana (APY) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance, administered by PFRDA
**Scheme ID:** atal-pension-yojana | **Category:** Pension / Social Security

## Overview
Atal Pension Yojana (launched 9 May 2015) guarantees a **fixed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month from age 60** for unorganised-sector workers, with 5 slabs. Government of India co-contribution existed for early joiners (2015–2016); now it is a subscriber-funded scheme with guaranteed pension.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. **Age 18–40 years** at enrolment (contribution continues till 60).
2. Must have a **savings bank account** (auto-debit mandate required).
3. Applicant should **not be** an income-tax payer (per scheme norms) and should not be covered under any statutory social security scheme (EPF/NPS by employer); aimed at unorganised-sector workers.
4. Aadhaar-linked account + mobile number for mandate & KYC.

## Benefits
- Guaranteed pension ₹1,000/₹2,000/₹3,000/₹4,000/₹5,000 per month from age 60, for life.
- On subscriber's death: **spouse continues** receiving the same pension; after both, the **nominee receives the accumulated corpus**.
- Contribution amounts vary by entry age and slab — e.g., a subscriber joining at 18 for the ₹5,000 slab pays ~₹210/month.

## Application Process
1. Approach any bank branch / net-banking / mobile app offering APY.
2. Fill the APY registration form; choose pension slab + auto-debit frequency (monthly/quarterly/half-yearly/annual).
3. Aadhaar + mobile linked; mandate registered; contributions begin.
4. Track via bank or NSDL–CRA APY portal (npscra.nsdl.co.in/apy-form.php); PRAN issued.
5. Pension starts at 60; scheme is portable across banks/jobs.
