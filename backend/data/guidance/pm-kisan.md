# PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
**Scheme ID:** pm-kisan | **Category:** Agriculture / Income Support

## Overview
PM-KISAN provides **₹6,000 per year per family** in three equal installments of ₹2,000, credited directly to the bank accounts of landholding farmer families. Launched Feb 2019; benefits flow every 4 months.

## Eligibility Criteria (Revised Operational Guidelines)
A **landholding farmer family** — husband, wife, and minor children — with cultivable land in their name (as per state land records) is eligible, subject to these **exclusions** (any one applies → excluded):

1. All **institutional landholders** (farmer families holding institutional land).
2. Former and present **holders of constitutional posts**; former/present **Ministers/MLAs/MLCs/Mayors/District Panchayat Presidents** and similar.
3. **Gazetted or serving/retired government employees** (Centre/State/PSU/Autonomous bodies — Class I/II and equivalent regular employees; Class III/IV with conditions per latest guidelines).
4. **Income-tax payers** in the last assessment year (self or spouse).
5. Persons with **professional registration** — doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects (practising).
6. **NRI farmers** (per Income Tax Act definitions) — excluded.

## Benefit
₹2,000 × 3 installments/year via DBT (Aadhaar-linked account). eKYC is mandatory before credit.

## Application Process
1. Register at pmkisan.gov.in → "New Farmer Registration" (or via CSC).
2. Aadhaar, land record details, bank account.
3. eKYC via Aadhaar OTP; land verification by state revenue officials.
4. Village- level verification → approval → DBT.
5. Track "Beneficiary Status" on the portal; correct via "Farmers Corner".
