# PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's Atma Nirbhar Nidhi) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
**Scheme ID:** pm-svanidhi | **Category:** Livelihood / Micro-credit

## Overview
PM SVANidhi gives urban street vendors collateral-free **working capital loans**: ₹10,000 (1st tranche) → ₹20,000 (2nd) → ₹50,000 (3rd), with **7% interest subsidy on timely repayment** and **₹1,200/year cashback** on digital transactions (100 × ₹12). Launched June 2020, extended till Dec 2025+.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. **Street vendor/hawker** vending in an **urban area** — on or before **24 March 2020** (identification: Certificate of Vending / ULB survey) — or specific categories per current relaxed norms (LoR route for vendors on municipal land, in vending zones identified later).
2. **18+ years**.
3. Has a **bank account** (preferably Aadhaar-linked; mandatory for subsidy).
4. Vendors from surrounding peri-urban areas of the ULB are also included on identification.

## Exclusions
- Pure rural vendors (not under ULB) are outside this scheme.
- Regular employees of any organisation moonlighting as vendors on the side are not targeted.

## Benefits
1. First loan ₹10,000 (1-year tenure); on-time repayment unlocks ₹20,000; then ₹50,000 (3 & 5-year options).
2. 7% p.a. interest subsidy credited quarterly on timely repayment.
3. ₹100/month cashback on digital payments (up to 12 months = ₹1,200) via UPI/Bharat QR.
4. No collateral, no processing guarantee fee; moratorium options per lender norms.

## Application Process
1. Get **Certificate of Vending (CoV)** from ULB, or a **Letter of Recommendation (LoR)** (for pre-2020 vendors missing from surveys; ULB-issued).
2. Apply at **pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in**, any lending institution (SBI/other PSBs, NBFCs, MFIs, SHG-bank), or CSC.
3. Documents: Aadhaar, CoV/LoR, bank details, photo.
4. Loan disbursal into bank account (typically ~7–30 days).
5. Repay on schedule → next tranche + interest subsidy DBT.
