# PM Vishwakarma — Guidance

**Ministries:** MSME + Ministry of Skill Development (MSDE)
**Scheme ID:** pm-vishwakarma | **Category:** Livelihood / Artisan Credit

## Overview
PM Vishwakarma (launched 17 Sep 2023) provides end-to-end support to **artisans and craftspeople** of **18 traditional trades**: (1) Carpenter (Suthar), (2) Boat Maker, (3) Armourer, (4) Blacksmith (Lohar), (5) Hammer & Tool Kit Maker, (6) Potter (Kumhaar), (7) Cobbler (Charmakar), (8) Mason (Rajmistri), (9) Stone-carver/Stone-breaker, (10) Cloth/Dhoti/Banner Stitcher (Darzi), (11) Tailor, (12) Garland Maker (Malakar), (13) Bamboo Worker (Baskar), (14) Broom Maker, (15) Fisher (Dhimar), (16) Doll/Toy Maker (Traditional), (17) Barber (Nai), (18) Washerman (Dhobi), plus Goldsmith (Sunar) variants per the official 18-trade list.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Applicant is **18+ years** on registration date.
2. Working **with hands and tools** in one of the **18 notified trades**, in the unorganised sector (self-employed), rural or urban.
3. Minimum 5 years of engagement in the trade (basic skill familiarity; self-declaration with verification).
4. **Not availing** similar credit-linked benefits from other central schemes (one per family norms apply; Govt employees/family excluded; income-tax payers excluded).

## Benefits
1. **Recognition**: PM Vishwakarma certificate + ID card (after verification) + ₹500 initial digital incentive for registration.
2. **Skilling**: 5–7 days basic training + 15 days advanced training (₹500/day stipend).
3. **Toolkit incentive**: ₹15,000 (80% as e-voucher + 20% on toolkit receipt).
4. **Collateral-free enterprise loans**: ₹1 lakh first tranche (18-month repayment) → ₹2 lakh second tranche (30 months) at **5% concessional interest**, no collateral.
5. **Digital transactions incentive**: ₹1 per transaction (up to 100/month).
6. **Marketing support**: e-commerce onboarding, brand "PM Vishwakarma" linkage.

## Application Process
1. Register at **pmvishwakarma.gov.in** or nearest CSC (₹ fee minimal).
2. Aadhaar + trade details + self-declaration of trade experience.
3. Multi-level verification: Gram Panchayat/ULB → District Committee → State.
4. After verification: recognition certificate + ID card issued; enrol for training.
5. On training completion → toolkit incentive; loan application via linked bank (SBI and partner banks).
