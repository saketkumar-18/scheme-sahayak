# PM Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
**Scheme ID:** pmay-u-2-0 | **Category:** Housing | **Period:** 2024–2030

## Overview
PMAY-U 2.0 ("Housing for All" continuation) assists urban households who are homeless or living in kutcha houses to buy or build their first pucca house. It runs through verticals including in-situ rehabilitation of slums (ISS), Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP), and the Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS-CLSS style) on home loans via Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs).

## Income Categories (household income per year)
- EWS (Economically Weaker Section): up to ₹3 lakh
- LIG (Low Income Group): ₹3–6 lakh
- MIG (Middle Income Group): ₹6–9 lakh (PMAY-U 2.0 consolidated MIG ceiling)

## Eligibility Criteria
1. The household must be in an **urban area** (statutory town coverage).
2. The beneficiary family must **not own a pucca house** anywhere in India (husband/wife/unmarried children count as one household).
3. Household income must fall within EWS/LIG/MIG ceilings — for the interest-subsidy vertical, EWS/LIG get subsidy up to ₹1.80 lakh on loans up to 20–23 year tenure.
4. Beneficiary must have a valid Aadhaar; the house is issued in the name of a female member or joint ownership where possible.

## Exclusions
- Families that already own a pucca house (self or through any family member).
- Persons convicted or facing charges for serious crimes (as per scheme norms).
- Institutional beneficiaries are not covered.

## Benefit
- Interest subsidy on home loans: up to ₹1.80 lakh (EWS/LIG), reducing effective interest burden; subsidy credited upfront to the loan account.
- AHP/ISS verticals: affordable houses with infrastructure at location.

## Application Process
1. Register at the PMAY-U 2.0 beneficiary portal (pmaymis.gov.in) — "Citizen Assessment" or new registration.
2. Aadhaar + bank details + income self-assessment.
3. Vertical selection: ISS (loan interest subsidy) or AHP (affordable house allotment).
4. Track status with the application ID.
5. For loan subsidy: approach any SCB/HFC — they route the subsidy via CBS integration.

## Key Documents
Aadhaar, income certificate/self-declaration, identity & address proof, bank passbook.
