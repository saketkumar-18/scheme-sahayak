# PM Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoRD)
**Scheme ID:** pmay-g | **Category:** Housing | **Coverage:** Rural India

## Overview
PMAY-G aims to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all rural households who are houseless or living in kutcha/dilapidated houses. Identification is primarily through SECC-2011 and the Awaas+ survey permanent wait lists managed at the Gram Panchayat level.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Household in a **rural area**.
2. **Houseless or living in a kutcha house** (SECC 2011 deprivation criteria D1/D2/D3 or Awaas+ survey).
3. **No family member owns a pucca house** anywhere in India.
4. No family member is an income-tax payer or holds a government job (priority scoring; not absolute bars at all state implementations, but core targeting).

## Benefit
- Financial assistance for new construction: approximately ₹1.2 lakh to ₹2 lakh per house depending on state and terrain (90:10 Centre:State in NE/hilly states, 60:40 in plains; unit size 25 sqm).
- 95% of funds via DBT to Aadhaar-linked accounts in installments (geo-tagged construction stages).

## Application Process
1. Check your name in the SECC-2011/Awaas+ permanent waitlist at the Gram Panchayat or on pmayg.nic.in.
2. If not listed, request the Gram Panchayat for inclusion during surveys.
3. Sanction order issued after verification; geo-tagging of construction stages.
4. Payments via AwaasSoft/PFMS in 3–4 installments.
5. Track: pmayg.nic.in → "Track Your Application".

## Key Documents
Aadhaar, bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked), ration card, SECC acknowledgement.
