# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) — Guidance

**Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development (NSAP division)
**Scheme ID:** nsap-old-age | **Category:** Pension / Social Assistance

## Overview
Under the **National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)**, IGNOAPS provides central assistance for old-age pensions to **BPL seniors**: ₹200/month for ages 60–79 and ₹500/month for 80+ (central share; most states add top-ups of ₹200–₹2,000+, e.g., Delhi ₹2,000+, varying by state). Paid via DBT, typically quarterly.

## Eligibility Criteria
1. Applicant is **60 years or older**.
2. Belongs to a **BPL household** (as per state BPL list / SECC-based criteria).
3. The applicant (or family) is not receiving any other state pension for the same purpose (double-pension restrictions vary by state).

## Benefits
- Central assistance: ₹200/month (60–79), ₹500/month (80+).
- State top-ups push total pension to ₹200–₹2,000+/month depending on state.
- DBT quarterly to Aadhaar-linked bank/post-office account.

## Application Process
1. Get the NSAP application form from the Block Development Office / Municipal office / Gram Panchayat (also on nsap.nic.in).
2. Attach: age proof (birth certificate/school certificate/voter ID), BPL/ration card, bank details, Aadhaar.
3. Sanction by the District Social Welfare/Panchayat officer after verification (Gram Sabha in rural areas).
4. Pension starts after sanction; DBT quarterly (back-dated to sanction month per state rules).
5. Track on nsap.nic.in → "Track Your Pension"; grievances via the state social welfare department helpline.

## Related NSAP Pensions
- **IGNWPS** (widow pension, 40–79, BPL): ₹300/month central + state top-ups.
- **IGNDPS** (disability pension, 18–79, 40%+ disability, BPL): ₹300/month central.
